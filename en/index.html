Univé — Digital products2022

Together with Yellowtail Conclusion I worked on a number of new (digital) products for Univé during a discovery project. The first product is about applying for term life insurance and the second about disability insurance. Univé wanted tools that let visitors see what their financial situation would be in the event of death or incapacity for work.

Eneco — Retail websites2023

Commissioned by Eneco, I was involved in building a multi-label Digital Experience Platform (DXP). This platform makes it possible to develop sales products for the Eneco, Oxxio and WoonEnergie brands. Eneco’s design system was connected to it, so that all products share the same look & feel and most components are reusable and scalable across all brands and digital channels. My role as a UX designer was to create multi-label designs for various (sales) flows and hand them over to the development teams.

ASN Bank — Platform ‘Zo maakt geld gelukkig’2021 – 2022

Zo maakt geld gelukkig (‘This is how money makes you happy’) is ASN Bank’s digital inspiration platform. On the platform, visitors find articles, blogs and videos about how money makes you happy. My goal as a UX designer was to upgrade the existing, static platform into a dynamic and inspiring content platform. In this case you can read how I approached that while working for de Volksbank.

Triodos — Mortgage journey2020

Commissioned by Yellowtail Conclusion, I worked on the new online mortgage journey for Triodos. Their previous journey was badly outdated: it had a long onboarding process, information was hard to find and the ‘Triodos feeling’ was missing. On top of that, it wasn’t responsive. Plenty of work to do. A nice bonus was that Triodos was rolling out a new visual identity at the time, so we were able to use and develop that new style in the new mortgage journey.

De Hypotheker — Mortgage file2020

Commissioned by Yellowtail Conclusion, I worked on a mortgage file environment for customers of De Hypotheker. The previous version was outdated and not responsive. A nice bonus was that De Hypotheker was rolling out a new visual identity at the time, so we were able to use and develop that new style in the new environment.

RegioBank — Website optimisation2021 – 2022

RegioBank calls itself the neighbourly bank and wants to be there for its customers. Because the bank wanted to reach a new target group, become commercially fitter and more service-oriented online, I was asked as a UX designer to refresh the look & feel of their current website. This meant redesigning components, creating a new visual style and developing new features. A great project I carried out while working for de Volksbank.

Van Gelder — E-commerce platform2017

Welcome to the wonderful world of fruit and vegetables, I thought when I started this project. Van Gelder groente & fruit is a fresh produce supplier, mainly active in the hospitality sector. For more than 65 years, this family business has been all about delivering fresh potatoes, vegetables and fruit every day. I was involved as a UX designer in this project, in which I got to design a platform in a completely new world and with its own visual style.

Salut (VGZ) — App design2020

The health app Salut (part of VGZ) makes a user’s health measurable. The app connects data and feelings, letting users indicate how good they feel. I was brought in as a visual designer by Yellowtail Conclusion during the discovery phase of the project. Together with an interaction designer from Yellowtail and the Salut project group, I mainly contributed to the app’s visual style.

Ockto — App design2022

With the Ockto app you retrieve personal data from government bodies (such as the Tax Administration or the UWV). You may need this data when applying for a mortgage, renting a home or leasing a car. The app makes sure this happens digitally and securely, so you can share your data with the right parties in no time. Ockto asked me to develop the app design for both iOS and Android in Figma and to link all components used to the design system (also in Figma).

DAK — Mortgage portal2020 – 2021

DAK Intermediairscollectief is a collective of and for 1,500 independent advisers in insurance, mortgages and loans. DAK asked Yellowtail Conclusion to develop an online mortgage environment for processing mortgage applications faster, more easily and above all more smartly. The goal was to build a complete tool that would be simple to use and contain all the functionality intermediaries and advisers need to manage their mortgages. In this case you can read how we approached it.

MDT Sport — New brand identity and website2020

MDT is a national programme of the Dutch Ministry of Health, Welfare and Sport and four major sports federations. MDT stands for Maatschappelijke DienstTijd (Social Community Service) and is a journey of discovery for young people towards the best version of themselves: a chance to discover their talents, be meaningful, meet new people and make choices for the future. MDT Sport focuses on the sports sector, in collaboration with the KNVB (football), KNKV (korfball), NeVoBo (volleyball) and KNLTB (tennis).

What I do

01

Product design

End-to-end UX and UI for apps, websites and portals: from user flows and wireframes to development-ready screens, built on a scalable design system and tested with real users.

02

UX design

User research, customer needs and interaction design. I make complex processes understandable and accessible, so users reach their goal without having to think.

03

Service design

I look beyond the screen: the entire customer journey across channels, including the processes and systems behind it. So app, website, customer service and back office fit together.

04

From idea to prototype

From a vague idea to a clickable prototype tested with real users, in a matter of weeks. With AI I make several directions tangible at high speed.

05

Facilitation & AI products

I guide teams and decision-makers through workshops towards shared decisions, and help design AI features that users understand, trust and can steer.

06
