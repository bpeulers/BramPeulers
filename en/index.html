What I do

01

Product design

End-to-end UX and UI for apps, websites and portals: from user flows and wireframes to development-ready screens, built on a scalable design system and tested with real users.

02

UX design

User research, customer needs and interaction design. I make complex processes understandable and accessible, so users reach their goal without having to think.

03

Service design

I look beyond the screen: the entire customer journey across channels, including the processes and systems behind it. So app, website, customer service and back office fit together.

04

From idea to prototype

From a vague idea to a clickable prototype tested with real users, in a matter of weeks. With AI I make several directions tangible at high speed.

05

Facilitation & AI products

I guide teams and decision-makers through workshops towards shared decisions, and help design AI features that users understand, trust and can steer.

06

Sectors & domains

Energy is my home base: usage insight, dynamic pricing, solar panels and payment processes. Plus experience in finance, insurance, healthcare, e-commerce and retail.
