Facilitator · AI prototyping · Product design

More director than maker.I use AI to make ideastangible at speed.

I’m Bram Peulers, freelance product designer and owner of De Merkenstudio. After more than ten years of designing apps, websites and digital environments, I increasingly see myself as a director rather than a maker.

My strength lies in the combination. I guide teams and decision-makers through the process, from the first brainstorm to the final decision, and at the same time use AI to make whatever is on the table tangible at speed. No waiting weeks for a design, but a prototype to react to within the same week. That combination of facilitator and fast builder is still fairly rare.

Over the years I have worked in sectors where a lot is at stake and processes are complex: energy, finance, insurance, healthcare, e-commerce, retail and government. I know how regulated organisations work, how to design around existing systems and what it takes to get business, IT and customer aligned. That domain knowledge means I get up to speed quickly and ask the right questions, in every new organisation.

I’m also going deeper into designing AI products themselves. How do you design trust, errors and control into AI features? There is still little expertise there, and that is exactly where I want to make a difference.

From vague idea to tested prototype

An indicative track of a few weeks. Every assignment is different; the rhythm stays the same.

  1. Week 1 · Getting sharp together — workshop with team and decision-makers: what is the problem, for whom, and when is it a success?
  2. Week 1–2 · Making it tangible fast — using AI to build several directions as a clickable prototype
  3. Week 2–3 · Testing and choosing — validating with real users and choosing a direction together with decision-makers
  4. Week 3–4 · Capturing and handing over — working out the chosen concept, ready for development
