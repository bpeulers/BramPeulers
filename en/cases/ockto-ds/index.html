Ockto —Design system

ClientOckto
RoleUX designer
Period2022
TypeDesign systems

With the Ockto app you retrieve personal data from government bodies (such as the Tax Administration or the UWV). You may need this data when applying for a mortgage, renting a home or leasing a car. The app makes sure this happens digitally and securely, so you can share your data with the right parties in no time. Ockto asked me to develop a design system in Figma that acts as a central source and is linked to all of the app’s designs.

Case

The Ockto Design System is a central place describing components, graphic styles and UX elements such as colours, fonts, buttons, forms, headers, footers and logos. This digital library in Figma is linked to all Ockto designs. Besides the general Ockto elements, the library also covers white-label apps, iOS and Android components and dark mode.

“Our company recently migrated from Sketch/Zeplin to Figma as our design tool. De Merkenstudio took care of the entire migration, including a complete design system and streamlined designs across both app platforms. Our team really enjoys working with Figma!

We also regularly bring in De Merkenstudio for mobile app designs of new features. I find the sessions with Bram very pleasant. Bram is strong at translating our requirements into mobile designs and has a good eye for our product and vision.

I can also always turn to him with questions about using Figma, certain design considerations or small changes to our design system. In short, a really great collaboration with De Merkenstudio!”

Martine Laan – Ockto

The result

Creating a design system for Ockto brings many benefits, such as: consistency across all digital channels, managing components and elements, maintaining templates and style guides from one library, adapting and creating designs faster, finding the right information faster, and more efficient alignment between designers, information analysts and developers.

Finally

I’m a big fan of design systems. By capturing all graphic elements and components in a central library, you create a consistent user experience across all channels and enable design and development teams to work together efficiently. But setting up such a system properly is often a big job. That’s why I’m proud that we managed to create a new design system for Ockto from scratch.
