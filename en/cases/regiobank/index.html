RegioBank —Website optimisation

ClientRegioBank
RoleUX/UI designer
Period2021 – 2022
TypeWebsites

RegioBank calls itself the neighbourly bank and wants to be there for its customers. Because the bank wanted to reach a new target group, become commercially fitter and more service-oriented online, I was asked as a UX designer to refresh the look & feel of their current website. This meant redesigning components, creating a new visual style and developing new features. A great project I carried out while working for de Volksbank.

Case

RegioBank wants to become commercially fitter online by attracting more prospects and retaining visitors. It also wants to attract a new target group, ‘the new conservatives’ (financially fit people over 50 with an interest in the stock market), who should be inspired with relevant content across RegioBank’s channels. Finally, RegioBank wants to become more service-oriented online: helping visitors find relevant information quickly and offering a helping hand as soon as a visitor has questions.

A big advantage was that we could build on the existing pages of RegioBank.nl. There was also plenty of data in Adobe Analytics for our analysis. The project was mainly about redesigning the current website, which made it a great challenge for me as a UX designer. Because we wanted to substantiate our choices, we organised a user test with RegioBank customers in which we tested a clickable demo of the redesign.

The process

The project consisted of the following phases: the brainstorm phase, the design phase, user research and the handover to front-end. I was involved as a UX designer in every phase and could apply my knowledge and skills.

Brainstorm phase

The brainstorm phase was mainly about gathering background knowledge, organising a target group analysis, setting KPIs and benchmarking inside and outside the market. In the creative meetings I had stakeholders actively think along and come up with ideas for the new platform.

Design phase

In the design phase I made various sketches of the new web pages and had them reviewed by stakeholders and UX colleagues. After several feedback rounds, we chose to turn the designs into a clickable prototype in InVision to give as realistic a picture as possible during the user test.

We designed various new pages, such as the homepage, life event pages, landing pages, service pages and article pages, which I designed for different devices (mobile, tablet and desktop). While designing, the project group mainly considered how to inspire users and help them find relevant information quickly. We gave components a new look & feel that later also made its way into the Volksbank design system.

User research

During the user research I was involved as a UX designer in setting up the test (formulating objectives and questions) and taking notes during the customer interviews.

Handover to development

In the final phase (the handover) my work consisted of presenting the designs, explaining the choices and handing the designs over to the development team (through reviews, refinements and sprints). Documenting the components and my design choices was also part of this phase.

Finally

The RegioBank redesign is currently being built by the retail team and is not yet live. It was a challenging project for me, because I could apply my skills as a consultant, UX designer and visual designer.
