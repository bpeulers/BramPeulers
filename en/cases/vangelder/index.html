Van Gelder —E-commerce platform

ClientVan Gelder
RoleUX designer
Period2017
TypeWebsites

Welcome to the wonderful world of fruit and vegetables, I thought when I started this project. Van Gelder groente & fruit is a fresh produce supplier, mainly active in the hospitality sector. For more than 65 years, this family business has been all about delivering fresh potatoes, vegetables and fruit every day. I was involved as a UX designer in this project, in which I got to design a platform in a completely new world and with its own visual style.

Case

Van Gelder asked CloudSuite to design an inspiration platform showing customer-specific information about range, delivery dates, availability and prices, without compromising performance. A platform where customers are inspired through attractive landing pages, blogs, videos and much more. That is something we took into account in the design. The distinctive footer, for example, looks inspiring. Another example is the blog pages with recipes that link to specific products. Before logging in no prices are shown, and after logging in they are, so that only their (hospitality) customers can order products.

1 / 2

Process

First of all, it was great to be invited to Van Gelder itself, to see with my own eyes how the wholesale business works and how the fruit and vegetables are distributed. After a number of creative sessions I then started as a UX designer on the most important pages, such as the homepage, product pages, order page and blog pages.

In the final phase (the handover) my work consisted of presenting the designs, explaining the choices and handing the designs over to the development team (through reviews, refinements and sprints). Documenting the components and my design choices was also part of this phase.

1 / 3

The result

Creating a new e-commerce platform for Van Gelder was a challenging but also very enjoyable project for me, because the product had to be designed from scratch and the fruit and vegetable trade was a completely new world to me. For me it has truly become a success now that 98.4% of all orders go through the web shop.

Feel free to take a look at www.vangeldernederland.nl.
