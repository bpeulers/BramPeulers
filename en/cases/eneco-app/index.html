Eneco —App team

ClientEneco
RoleProduct designer
Period2023
TypeApps

At Eneco I work as a product designer in the app team, developing new products for the app. Eneco’s app design system is connected to this, so that all components and screens in the app share the same look & feel and most components are reusable and scalable across all brands and digital channels. My role is to organise brainstorms with stakeholders, create multi-label designs for the Eneco apps and hand them over to the development teams.

1 / 7

Dynamic pricing

Dynamic pricing is a new theme within Eneco and stands for dynamic energy contracts. When I joined the app team the product was still in its infancy, and it is up to my fellow UX designers and me to take it to the next level. Together with marketers and the product owner I organise meetings in which stakeholders think along about the design and the prototypes are presented. In these sessions we define the overall product goals, map customer needs and test prototypes in the UX lab.

App design system

For our products we use Eneco’s app design system as much as possible. This creates uniformity across platforms (websites and apps). It also lets designers and developers work together faster, because they speak the same language.

App of the future

With the ‘app of the future’ concept we want to bring two Eneco apps together (the regular app and the dynamic pricing app). This prototype is meant to inspire and to kick off the discussion about a new app.
At the same time we want to make important updates in this new concept, such as a new structure (more layering), a new onboarding (clean & simple), the use of smart content and a new look & feel. The concept is currently in the research phase, and several features are already on the development backlog.
