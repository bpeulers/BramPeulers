Essent —Digital customer journeys I Use & I Pay

ClientEssent
RoleUX designer
Period2026 – present
TypeApps

At Essent I work as a UX designer on two key customer journeys: I Use and I Pay. I improve the digital energy environment and design user-friendly solutions for payment processes, translating complex information and business processes into clear, accessible digital experiences.

I Use – Insight into energy usage

Within I Use I work on a renewed energy environment in which customers gain more insight into their usage and energy generation.

My work:

  • Designing new dashboards and usage screens
  • Integrating real-time P1 data
  • Visualising solar energy, feed-in and self-consumption
  • Developing a new navigation structure for electricity, gas, costs and solar panels
  • Prototyping and user research

The goal: translating complex energy data into understandable insights that help customers use energy more wisely.

I Pay – Payments and contracts

Within I Pay I improve the customer journey around payments, reminders and welcome discounts.

One concrete project is optimising the welcome bonus process for MediaMarkt customers, where a discount is part of the energy contract.

My work:

  • Designing user flows for application and payout
  • Mapping different customer and cancellation scenarios
  • Improving communication about payments and terms
  • Aligning UX solutions with underlying systems such as SAP and Cockpit
  • Working with product owners, stakeholders and developers

The challenge: translating complex financial and operational processes into a clear and predictable customer experience.

Complexity & challenges

Both journeys require translating complex information and processes into simple solutions.

  • Making energy data understandable and visually accessible
  • Bringing together different customer needs and scenarios
  • Taking technical dependencies and existing systems into account
  • Balancing customer needs, business and technical feasibility

Approach & collaboration

From analysis to delivery I work closely with product owners, developers and stakeholders. I organise brainstorm sessions, facilitate discussions and regularly spar with the people involved to sharpen ideas, share insights and arrive at solutions everyone supports.

  1. Analysing existing customer journeys and user needs
  2. Organising brainstorms and co-creation sessions
  3. Translating insights into UX concepts and prototypes in Figma
  4. Validating solutions with users and stakeholders
  5. Turning designs into feasible solutions

Result & impact

  • Innovative concepts for the digital energy environment
  • More insight into energy usage and generation
  • Improved user flows for payment processes
  • Better alignment between stakeholders, business and development
  • User-friendly solutions that connect customer needs and business goals
